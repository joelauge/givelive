## The Shift from Paper to Digital: Why It Matters

Connection cards have long been a staple in churches, serving as a means to gather visitor information and engage with congregants. However, as technology evolves, many churches are finding that **QR codes** offer a modern, efficient alternative. In this article, we will explore how churches can transition from traditional paper workflows to automated digital solutions using QR codes, enhancing visitor experience and communication.

### Why QR Codes for Connection? 

1. **Instant Access**: QR codes provide immediate access to forms and information without the need for physical interaction, reducing barriers for visitors who may be hesitant to fill out paper forms.
2. **Environmentally Friendly**: Moving away from paper reduces waste and aligns with sustainable practices.
3. **Data Capture and Analytics**: Digital forms can automatically capture data and integrate with your CRM, providing valuable insights into visitor demographics and engagement patterns.
4. **Follow-Up Automation**: QR codes can trigger automated follow-up messages via SMS or email, ensuring timely communication with new visitors.

### Setting Up Your QR Code Connection Flow

Here’s a step-by-step guide for church communications teams to implement a QR code system for connection cards:

#### Step 1: Choose Your QR Code Platform

Start by selecting a QR code solution that allows you to create customizable flows. **GiveLive** is an excellent choice as it offers integrations with various CRMs and marketing tools, making it easy to manage follow-ups and data capture.

#### Step 2: Create Your Connection Flow

Determine what information you want to capture from visitors. A typical connection flow might include:
- Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Preferred Contact Method
- Interest Areas (e.g., small groups, volunteer opportunities)

Using GiveLive, create a simple lead capture form that visitors will fill out after scanning the QR code. You can use our [New Member](/blog/new-member) template to get started with a welcoming flow.

#### Step 3: Generate Your QR Code

Once your lead capture form is ready, generate a QR code linking to it. Ensure the code is easily scannable and visually appealing—consider adding your church logo or a welcoming message.

#### Step 4: Promote Your QR Code

Display your QR codes prominently throughout your church. Here are some effective placements:
- **Welcome Desk**: Place a sign with the QR code where visitors first arrive.
- **Bulletins and Newsletters**: Include the QR code in printed materials.
- **Service Screens**: Show the QR code during services to encourage immediate action.

#### Step 5: Automate Follow-Ups

Set up an automated email or SMS response to thank visitors for filling out the form. You can personalize this message based on the information they provided. For example, if they expressed interest in small groups, include details about upcoming meetings. GiveLive allows for seamless integration with tools like Mailchimp or HubSpot for this purpose.

### Overcoming Challenges and Concerns

While transitioning to QR codes offers numerous benefits, some churches may face challenges, such as:
- **Tech-Savvy Barriers**: Ensure staff is trained on how to use QR code technology effectively. Offer a brief tutorial during staff meetings.
- **Resistance from Older Generations**: Provide support for those who may struggle with technology. Consider maintaining a paper option for those who prefer it, while encouraging QR code use.
