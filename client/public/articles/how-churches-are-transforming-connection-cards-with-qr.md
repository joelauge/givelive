## Introduction

Connection cards have long been a staple for churches, serving as a primary method for collecting visitor information and fostering community engagement. However, as technology evolves, many churches are finding that paper-based workflows are not only outdated but also inefficient. By implementing QR codes, churches can transform their connection card process into a seamless digital experience that resonates with today’s visitors.

In this guide, we will explore how churches can replace traditional connection cards with QR codes and the advantages that come with this shift. We’ll also provide practical steps and tools, including how to use GiveLive for effective implementation.

## The Limitations of Paper Connection Cards

While connection cards can be effective, they come with notable challenges:
- **Manual Data Entry**: Staff members must manually enter visitor information into their systems, which is time-consuming and prone to errors.
- **Lost Opportunities**: Paper cards can easily be misplaced or forgotten, resulting in missed connections with potential newcomers.
- **Lack of Engagement**: Visitors may feel less inclined to fill out a paper card, especially if they have to wait in line or if there is no immediate follow-up.

These limitations highlight the need for a more efficient and engaging process.

## Why Visitors Prefer QR Codes

Adopting QR codes offers a range of benefits that align with the preferences of modern visitors:
- **Instant Access**: By simply scanning a QR code with their smartphones, visitors can quickly access the connection form without the hassle of paper and pens.
- **Easy Follow-Up**: Automated follow-ups via SMS or email can be integrated into the QR flow, ensuring that visitors feel welcomed and informed after their visit.
- **Data Accuracy**: Information is captured digitally, reducing the risk of errors that often occur during manual data entry.
- **Customization**: QR flows can be tailored to specific campaigns, such as welcoming newcomers or promoting events, making the experience more relevant and engaging.
