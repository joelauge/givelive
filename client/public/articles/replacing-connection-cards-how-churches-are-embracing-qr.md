## Introduction

Connection cards have long been a staple in churches, providing a way for visitors to share their information and get connected. However, as digital engagement continues to rise, many churches are recognizing the limitations of paper workflows. Enter QR codes—a simple yet powerful tool that can streamline the connection process, reduce manual entry, and enhance visitor experience.

## Why QR Codes?

1. **Instant Access**: QR codes allow visitors to access forms and information instantly using their smartphones, eliminating the need for physical cards.
2. **Data Collection**: Automated systems collect data in real-time, reducing the risk of errors associated with manual entry.
3. **Follow-Up Opportunities**: With integrated follow-up solutions, churches can engage visitors more effectively than with traditional methods.
4. **Sustainable Practices**: Going digital supports environmental sustainability by reducing paper waste.

## Transitioning from Paper to Digital

### Step 1: Assess Your Current Workflow
Take a moment to evaluate how your church currently collects visitor information. Identify the following:
- How many visitors fill out connection cards each week?
- What information are you collecting?
- How do you follow up with visitors after their first visit?

### Step 2: Choose Your QR Code Solution
Look for a QR code platform that meets your needs. **GiveLive** offers a user-friendly solution that allows you to create customized QR flows for various church activities. You can set up:
- New member sign-ups ([New Member](/blog/new-member))
- Baptism registrations ([Get Baptised](/blog/get-baptised))
- Small group connections ([Small Groups](/blog/small-groups))

### Step 3: Create Your QR Flows
Using GiveLive, design your QR flows that guide visitors to the necessary forms. For example, for new visitors, create a “Welcome to Our Church” flow that:
- Captures basic information (name, email, phone number)
- Provides a brief overview of church programs
- Offers an option to subscribe to newsletters

### Step 4: Test Your QR Codes
Before introducing QR codes to your congregation, test them yourself:
- Scan the QR code with multiple devices to ensure compatibility.
- Check the user experience and make adjustments if necessary.
- Ensure that the data integrates seamlessly with your CRM system (like HubSpot or Salesforce).

### Step 5: Educate Your Congregation
When rolling out QR codes, communication is key. Make sure to:
- Explain how the QR codes work during services.
- Provide signage around the church with clear instructions on how to use them.
- Encourage staff and volunteers to assist visitors in using QR codes during services.

### Step 6: Monitor and Adjust
Once your QR codes are in place, monitor their effectiveness:
- Track the number of leads captured through the QR codes.
- Analyze the data to see which flows are most popular and adjust your strategies accordingly.
- Use feedback from visitors to improve the experience continuously.

## Benefits of Going Digital

- **Increased Engagement**: Studies consistently show that people respond better to digital forms over paper forms, leading to higher engagement rates.
- **Timely Follow-ups**: Automated follow-ups through email or SMS can nurture connections with visitors, enhancing their chances of returning.
- **Data-Driven Insights**: With integrated CRM systems, you can gain valuable insights into visitor demographics and preferences, helping you tailor your outreach effectively.
