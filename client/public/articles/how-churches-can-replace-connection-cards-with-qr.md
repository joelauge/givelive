## The Challenge with Paper Connection Cards

For many churches, connection cards have long been a staple for gathering visitor information, enabling follow-up, and fostering community engagement. However, these traditional paper forms often lead to a cumbersome workflow:

- **Manual entry** of visitor information into databases.
- **Lost cards** that lead to missed connections.
- **Inconsistent follow-up** due to time constraints.

By contrast, churches that leverage **QR codes** can streamline this process dramatically, making it easier for visitors to engage without the friction of paper forms.

## Why Visitors Prefer QR Codes

1. **Convenience**: Visitors can scan a QR code using their smartphones without needing to fill out a paper form.
2. **Speed**: The digital process is faster; visitors can complete their information in seconds.
3. **Privacy**: Many visitors appreciate the discretion of entering their details on their own devices.
4. **Environmentally Friendly**: Reducing paper waste aligns with many churches' values regarding stewardship of the earth.

### Real-World Example of QR Code Implementation

Take for instance a church that recently transitioned from paper connection cards to QR codes. They created a simple flow using GiveLive:

1. **QR Code Creation**: The church generated a unique QR code linked to a digital form collecting visitor information.
2. **Placement**: Codes were placed at strategic locations around the church, including on bulletins, at the entrance, and on screens during services.
3. **Automated Follow-up**: After scanning, visitors received a thank-you text and an invitation to a newcomer’s event via SMS.

This transition resulted in a **50% increase** in visitor information capture and a noticeable uptick in event attendance.
