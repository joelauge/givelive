## Introduction

Churches have long relied on connection cards to capture visitor information and facilitate engagement. These cards, typically filled out during services, often end up lost or forgotten, resulting in missed opportunities to connect with newcomers. However, **the rise of QR codes offers a modern and efficient alternative**. By leveraging QR-powered acquisition platforms like GiveLive, churches can streamline their communication and engagement processes. This guide will explore the practical steps for replacing traditional connection cards with QR codes, while highlighting the benefits visitors enjoy from this shift.

## The Limitations of Paper Connection Cards

While connection cards have served churches for years, they come with notable drawbacks:

- **Manual Entry**: Staff members often have to manually enter the data from these cards into a database. This is time-consuming and prone to errors.
- **Lost Opportunities**: Visitors might forget to fill them out or leave them behind, leading to lost connections.
- **No Follow-Up**: Even if a card is filled out, the lack of automated follow-up means many visitors slip through the cracks.

## The Advantages of QR Codes

Switching to QR codes brings several advantages:
- **Instant Access**: Visitors can scan a QR code with their smartphones to instantly access a digital connection form.
- **Automated Lead Capture**: Captured information is automatically stored in your CRM, eliminating manual data entry.
- **Follow-Up Automation**: With platforms like GiveLive, you can set up automated email and SMS follow-ups based on the information collected.

## How to Implement QR Codes in Your Church

Here’s a step-by-step guide for church communications teams to transition from paper connection cards to QR codes:

### Step 1: Choose a QR Code Platform
Select a QR code platform that integrates well with your existing systems. GiveLive offers a user-friendly interface that allows you to create customizable QR flows with ease. Consider starting with their free plan, which allows one campaign and up to 100 leads per month.

### Step 2: Create Your Digital Connection Form
Use GiveLive to create a digital connection form. This form should be simple and user-friendly, asking for essential information such as:
- Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- How they heard about your church

### Step 3: Generate Your QR Code
Once your form is set up, generate a QR code that links directly to it. Place this code prominently in your church:
- **Bulletins**: Add the QR code to your weekly bulletins.
- **Screens**: Display the QR code on screens during services.
- **Welcome Packs**: Include it in welcome packets for new visitors.

### Step 4: Communicate the Benefits
Let visitors know why they should use the QR code instead of a paper card. Highlight these points:
- **Convenience**: They can fill out the form on their phones at their own pace.
- **Privacy**: Many visitors prefer the anonymity of filling out a digital form.
- **Immediate Follow-Up**: Assure them they will receive a welcome message soon after submission.

### Step 5: Monitor and Optimize
After implementing QR codes, monitor the engagement metrics through your platform. Look for:
- **Submission Rates**: Track how many visitors are completing the digital form.
- **Follow-Up Success**: Measure the effectiveness of your follow-up emails and texts.
- **Adjusting Strategy**: If engagement is low, consider adjusting the messaging or placement of your QR codes.
